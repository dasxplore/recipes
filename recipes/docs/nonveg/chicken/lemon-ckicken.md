# 🍗 Lemon Chicken Recipe
This is really easy to cook, tasty chicken recipe which you can try at home. You won't need huge list of items for this. So, let's do this.

## 📜 Ingredients
You can change the proportion as per you need. Here `tbsp` is table spoon and `tsp` is tea spoon.

1. Chicken: 1kg
2. Curd or Yogurt: 200gm
3. Ginger garlic paste: 1 tbsp
4. Lemon juice: 2 tbsp
5. Salt: 1/2 tsp
6. Black pepper powder: 1 tbsp
7. Garam masala powder: 1 tsp
8. Optional: Some potatoes cut into medium blocks, some small slices of lemon and green chillies

## 👨‍🍳 Cooking Process

### Mix & Marinate

* Put the chicken into a bowl, add the curd (dahi) or yogurt, add the ginger garlic paste, lemon juice, pepper powder.

* Mix well, cover the bowl and put it to marinate for 30 min to 1 hour.

### Cook it

* After it is marinated for at least 30min, take a kadai / pan and put it on stove, heat a bit.

* Then put some little oil of your choice and make it little hot.

* Add some whole garam masalas like 3-4 cloves, 4-5 black pepper, 1 bay leaf (tez patta), 1 cinnamon, 1 tsp cumin (jeera), 2 cardamom (elaichi). Then stir it for few seconds in the oil.

* Take out the chicken pieces from the marination (do not take the mixture now) and put it on oil. You can use high flame here.

* Fry them a little brown by twisting them periodically.

* Then put the curd / yogurt mixture from the marination. You can use little water to clean the bowl & put it in the pan.

* Then put the cover of the pan and cook it well for 15-20 min in low flame. Periodically you can mix them.

#### Optional part

* You can take a small bowl, then put some corn starch/flour, add some water, mix well. Then you can put it in the pan to make a thicker gravy.

* You can add some chilli slices, coriander leaves, small portion of black pepper powder, some lemon slices and just mix them, then stop the flame.
