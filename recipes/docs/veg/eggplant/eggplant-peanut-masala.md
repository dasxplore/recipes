# 🍆 Eggplant Peanut Masala Recipe
A quick & tasty recipe of eggplant (Brinjal) with potato and peanut masala. Enjoy this delicious recipe at home. So, let's get started.

## 📜 Ingredients
1. One or two medium size eggplant / brinjal 🍆
2. One or two medium size potato 🥔
3. One medium size onion 🧅
4. One medium size tomato 🍅
5. 30-50gm roasted peanut 🥜
6. 10gm poppy seeds
7. Oil, salt, black cumin, dry red chilli, little garlic-ginger-chilli paste, little turmeric powder

## 👨‍🍳 Cooking Process

### Cut the Vegetables

* First cut the brinjal(s) into medium size pieces. It will look like small blocks.

* Then cut the potato(s) into cube like pieces.

* Cut the onion into really tiny pieces i.e. chopping.

* Cut the onion into small pieces. You can go for really smaller pieces.

### Make a Paste
Take the roasted peanuts and the poppy-seeds into a mixer grinder, add a little quantity of water. Simple mix well to make a creamy paste.

### Fry the Vegetables

* First fry the potatos in oil about 50% and take them out in a pot.

* Then fry the brinjal pieces. You can add a pinch of salt here. You need take them out into a pot before they get mashed as eggplant / brinjal is really soft veg.

### Making the full dish

* If you have any oil left on your pan you can then add the onions or you need to add little oil, heat a little and put your chooped onions. Make it little brown.

* Then add the ginger, garlic, green chilli paste, mix well.

* Then add the tomato pieces on top of it, put a little salt, stir well till the tomatoes melt down. Meanwhile you may add some salt, turmeric powder, little sugar (you may also avoid adding sugar).

* After that add the fried potatos and the peanut-poppy paste. Stir well and you can change the flame to medium.

* Once the oil starts to come out, add some water so that the potatoes can be bolied (soften), do not add too much.

* Once potatos are about 80% coocked, add the fried brinjal pieces, mix well. Put it into low flame.

* Finally once it is coocked well, it should be a thick gravy and should be a tasty meal which you can consume with rice or bread (roti).
